Financial Aid

Workforce Training programs partner with local business and industry partners to train residents of Utah and to further them in their careers. We offer public courses, pathway programs, and custom training to serve the needs of both organizations and individuals.

At Salt Lake Technical College, we have a dedicated Financial Aid Coordinator to help you navigate financing your education. Sandra Bright is available to discuss funding options, FAFSA questions, and any other questions you may have regarding financial aid. You can see her on campus or through virtual appointments.

Funding Sources/Initiatives

Short-Term Intensive Training

Short Term Intensive Training (STIT) funds are available for approved not-for-credit courses. If you are approved for funding, STIT may pay up to 50% of the cost of the tuition associated with the course. Funding is based on Utah Residency. If approved, you will receive instructions to register for the program you selected.

Application for Short Term Intensive Training (STIT) Funding - Salt Lake Community College

Utah Works

In partnership with Talent Ready Utah, Salt Lake Community College is proud to offer tuition-free training through Utah Works. These short-term trainings are designed to get you into the workforce fast with the skills Utah employers seek. Click here to see the programs covered by Utah Works and get started.

Custom Fit

PGrow, train and retain your current workforce with tailored trainings and funding provided by the Utah State Legislature. As the Salt Lake County Custom Fit provider, SLCC is here to help you pay for vital trainings to keep your business competitive. Up to 40% of training costs may be paid for your company! Click here to see the list of programs and if you’re qualified for Custom Fit Funding.

Strategic Workforce Initiative (SWI) Funding

Strategic Workforce Initiative funding may pay 50% or more of the cost for training in IT & Computer Application programs leading to high-demand careers. To apply for funding, complete the SWI application to determine your eligibility. Funding is based on Utah Residency. If approved, you will receive instructions to register for the program you selected.

Application: Strategic Workforce Initiative (SWI) Funding - Salt Lake Community College
