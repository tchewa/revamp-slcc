Financial Aid

Many SLTech programs are eligible for Pell Grants or VA Educational Benefits.(Note: Only US residents can get Pell Grants, FAFSA, and most scholarships.)

Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) online to get started.

At Salt Lake Technical College, we have a dedicated Financial Aid Coordinator to help you navigate financing your education. Sandra Bright is available to discuss funding options, FAFSA questions, and any other questions you may have regarding financial aid. You can see her on campus or through virtual appointments.

Scholarships & Grants

Technical Education Scholarship

The Technical Education Scholarship provides financial assistance to Utahns pursuing a technical education certificate in high demand industries. This scholarship is available to recent high school graduates and adults enrolled in a high demand program at Salt Lake Technical College. This state-sponsored scholarship program will cover tuition and fees for approved programs, provided you are making acceptable academic progress. We recommend students complete the 2024-2025 Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) for the upcoming academic year as well.

Technical Education Scholarship

Salt Lake Tech Scholarship

The SLTECH Scholarship provides financial assistance to students pursuing a technical education certificate in high demand industries at Salt Lake Technical College. This state-sponsored scholarship program covers tuition and fees for approved programs. To be eligible for this opportunity, students must apply and gain acceptance to Salt Lake Community College. Additionally, current SLTech students may also apply, provided they maintain acceptable academic progress in their respective programs. It's important to note that all applicants must have legal eligibility to work in the U.S. upon successful completion of their SLTech program.

Salt Lake Tech Scholarship

Questions about the application process should be directed to the Scholarship team and can be emailed to scholarships@slcc.edu.

ESL

English as a Second Language is eligible for the Mastering ESL Scholarship designed to financially assist students who are enrolled in English as a Second Language (ESL) courses at SLCC. This scholarship is worth up to $300 during the Fall semester. Questions about the application process should be directed to the ESL team at ESLAdmin@slcc.edu.
