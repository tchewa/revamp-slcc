Why Choose Salt Lake TECH?

Salt Lake Tech delivers technical education and skills-based training to cultivate the sought-after abilities desired by employers, catering to a diverse student body with varied educational and career goals.

Frequent Start Dates

Depending on program openings, you may start immediately

Flexible Scheduling

Arrange your class schedule around your lifestyle.

Low Cost

Technical education is a lower rate per credit hour. Scholarships may be available.

Career Ready

Our programs are tailored to meet the needs of business and industry and prepare you to enter the job market

Credit and Not-For-Credit Programs

Our programs provide academic credit applicable to degrees and short-term, non-credit options for gaining immediate job-ready skills.

Convenience

Salt Lake Tech courses are offered at 7 of the 10 SLCC locations

Academic Support

Need a little help with math, reading, ESL, or computer skills? Foundational courses are available.

Competency-based education

Move through competency-based education courses for some programs at an accelerated pace

Transferability

Depending on your program, some courses may transfer toward an associate's degree at SLCC
